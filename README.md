---

## 📖 About the Game

A child wanders into the woods and steps through a mysterious open gate.  
An evil witch awaits — she curses him, splits his shadow into an evil twin, and drains all color from the world.

**Your goal:** Collect the color shards and restore the world while keeping your fragile light alive.  
*Stay hidden from your own shadow… or be consumed by it.*
